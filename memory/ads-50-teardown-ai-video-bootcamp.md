Teardown of 50 ads at `~/Desktop/Ads/videos/` (sheets/ + txt/, built by [[ads-contact-sheet-pipeline]]). All 50 = ONE advertiser: **AI Video Bootcamp** — a Skool community, **$9/month**, teaches making AI video/influencers realistic enough to fool people, then monetize. ~760 ads total in their library = industry-scale brute-force testing. Used as the model FF's factory copies. Full factory plan: [[ai-ugc-ad-factory-workflow]].

## 4-beat skeleton (almost every clip)
1. **HOOK / AI-REVEAL (0-3s)**: "I'm not real, 100% AI" = pattern interrupt + curiosity loop.
2. **PROOF/DEMO (3-15s)**: show realism (skin/hands/lip-sync) + mundane routine (shopping/gym/cooking) + holding a brand product.
3. **OPPORTUNITY+OFFER (15-40s)**: "biggest opportunity of the era" + social proof (member count) + price anchor ($9 = one coffee).
4. **CTA**: "link below, $9/mo, sign up in 2 min" + Skool card (member count + JOIN $9/month).

## Hook formulas they actually test
- **AI self-reveal (~60%, dominant)**: "my face/voice all AI"
- **Realism challenge**: "zoom in on my pores/hands"
- **Myth-bust**: "everyone says check the hands — look, perfect"
- **Faceless story/confession**: "$2M influencer who doesn't exist" / "I've used AI 4 months, nobody noticed"
- **FOMO**: "every week you hesitate, someone gets 6 months ahead"
- **Mundane vlog**: "3am eating cold pasta in the dark, still thought I was real"
- **Niche**: real estate agent / brands-UGC / "be the face of your brand w/o anyone knowing"
- **Meta**: "this is an ad, and I'm the product"

## Visual
9:16 selfie talking-head AI avatar, home/car/gym setting (mundane = looks real). Word-by-word big center captions + "THIS VIDEO IS 100% AI" tag. CTA = Skool screenshot. Candid-real NOT glossy ("prompt for authenticity — real skin texture, messy hair, natural lighting").

## Persuasion levers
curiosity loop ("you couldn't tell, right?") · social proof escalating 1.7k→19k members · price anchor ($9 < coffee/Netflix) · objection kill (no tech/no camera needed) · risk reversal (money-back / cancel anytime) · outcome promise ("first AI video in 48hr").

## Length tiers
punch 14-30s (~40%) / mid 30-50s (most) / VSL 60-92s.

## THE scale mechanic (most important takeaway)
VSL body+CTA FIXED, test only HOOK + avatar. Clips #17/18/22/27 = **identical body script**, only opener/face/length differ → that's their **proven VSL** being scaled. The one variable that decides everything = first 3 seconds. This is the modular hook-swap engine FF's factory replicates.

## Caveats for copying (don't blindly clone)
- **Survivorship bias**: we see surviving ads, not winners (no CTR/CPA). Only reliable signal = repeated identical body (proven). Rest may just be in-test.
- They brute-force 760 ads on big budget; small budget must test fewer-but-smarter.
- **$9 coffee anchor / member counts = THEIR offer**, not transferable to a DFY agency. Don't clone numbers (false-claim + TH compliance risk).
- **AI-reveal angle saturating** — arriving late; need a twist tied to outcome/niche.
- These = Meta ads. TikTok TH needs faster hook (1s) + dirtier candid. "I'm not real" reveal unproven in Thai market (novelty for Westerners; may read as deceptive/uncanny to Thais — must test).
- TH compliance risk: health/income/guarantee claims.
