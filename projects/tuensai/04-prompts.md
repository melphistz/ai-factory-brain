Takeshi Kitano rhythm — static locked takes broken by sudden quick bursts, abrupt cuts. No dialogue.
Natural, genuine reactions throughout the rush — startled awake, flustered, focused, out of breath
— played real but never exaggerated or cartoonish. Save the deadpan for the very end.

silence; no music.
0–2s: she startles awake in bed in pajamas, genuinely alarmed, and scrambles up.
2–8s: a burst of rapid cuts — showering, brushing teeth, hurriedly buttoning her Thai school uniform
   (@Image1), snatching her bag — visibly flustered and in a real hurry.
8–10s: she runs down an empty sunlit street, getting out of breath.
10–12s: she reaches the school (@Image2) and stops dead — slightly panting, confused at the quiet,
   empty grounds.

   deadpan beat, then she lets out a small, tired sigh.
Natural skin, stable identity, no warping, no jitter.
